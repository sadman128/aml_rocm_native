Progress summary for your supervisor

Reproduction of Holla et al. (2025), "Adversarial Threats to Cloud IDS" — full pipeline implemented in PyTorch (paper used TensorFlow): baseline IDS → FGSM/PGD/DeepFool/Square Attack evaluation → dual-layer defense (adversarial training + SHAP-based robust feature selection) → re-evaluation. Running on full CSE-CIC-IDS2018 (all 10 daily files) via the cleaned Parquet mirror, now on ROCm (RX7600), after diagnosing and fixing several methodological issues along the way:

Equal training-budget bug — defended model was training 25 fewer epochs than baseline, making overhead falsely negative. Fixed; overhead now correctly positive (~20-30%, comparable to the paper's ~25%).
Attack-targeting bug — ART's attacks were targeting the model's own prediction instead of ground truth, artificially inflating accuracy under attack for the defended model. Fixed for FGSM/PGD (DeepFool has a structural limitation in ART that can't be fixed the same way — documented as a known caveat).
Dataset-scope issue — initial runs used only 3 of 10 daily files (missing Infiltration/Botnet/Web attacks), inflating baseline accuracy to 99.6% vs. the paper's 85%. Switched to all 10 files; baseline accuracy now closely matches the paper (85.8%).
DirectML → ROCm migration — measured 4.2x training speedup, cutting per-seed runtime from ~9hrs to ~3hrs, making a 3-seed stability study feasible overnight.
Seed-stability study in progress — first 3-seed run surfaced a real finding: the baseline model is not seed-stable (one seed collapsed to near-majority-class prediction, recall dropped from ~92% to ~38%), while the defended model was much more stable across seeds. Caught and fixed a confound before trusting this result: train/test split, SMOTE, and feature selection were all tied to the varying seed, meaning each "seed run" was secretly training on different data, not just different initialization. Now fixed with a DATA_SEED/SEED split so only model init and training stochasticity vary — re-running all 3 seeds tonight with the corrected isolation.
