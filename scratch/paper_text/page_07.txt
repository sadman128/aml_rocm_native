B. ATTACK STRATEGIES AND DEFENSE MECHANISMS
To evaluate the vulnerability of IDS models, we implemented
three adversarial attack techniques:
• FGSM (Fast Gradient Sign Method): Generates
adversarial samples by perturbing input features along
the gradient direction.
• PGD (Projected Gradient Descent): An iterative
variant of FGSM that applies multiple gradient updates
to maximize attack effectiveness.
• DeepFool: A sophisticated attack that iteratively per-
turbs samples until they cross the decision boundary of
the classifier.
To counter these attacks, we employed two defense
mechanisms:
• Adversarial Training: Incorporates adversarial exam-
ples into training to improve robustness.
• Robust Feature Selection: Identifies and prioritizes
stable features that contribute to model resilience against
adversarial manipulations.
