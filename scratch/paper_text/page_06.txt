E. ROBUST ADVERSARIAL TRAINING FRAMEWORK
Adversarial training has emerged as one of the most effective
techniques to enhance the robustness of ML-based IDS
against adversarial attacks. The core idea behind adversarial
training is to expose the IDS model to adversarially generated
samples during training, enabling it to learn patterns that
distinguish between genuine and manipulated network traffic.
This iterative process improves the model’s ability to gener-
alize under adversarial conditions, making it less susceptible
to evasion techniques.

F. ROBUST FEATURE SELECTION ALGORITHM
Selecting the right set of features plays a crucial role in
improving the resilience of IDS models against adversarial
attacks. Traditional feature selection techniques primarily
focus on selecting features that maximize classification
accuracy. However, adversarially robust feature selection
must prioritize stability, ensuring that the selected features
remain effective even under adversarial perturbations.

H. SIGNIFICANCE OF SHAP-BASED FEATURE SELECTION
SHAP analysis enables us to measure the consistency of
feature contributions across clean and adversarial datasets.
By filtering out features that experience high SHAP score
variations due to adversarial perturbations, we ensure that
only stable and robust features are retained. This approach
improves IDS resilience by:
• Identifying features that remain consistent under attack
conditions, reducing adversarial susceptibility.
• Enhancing model interpretability by providing insights
into feature stability.
• Improving generalization by selecting features that
retain predictive power across different attack scenarios.
By integrating adversarial training and robust feature selec-
tion, our proposed IDS framework significantly enhances
security against adversarial threats, ensuring improved
resilience in detecting and mitigating cyberattacks in cloud
environments.
IV. EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS
In this section, we present an in-depth evaluation of our
proposed adversarial training and robust feature selection
methods. We perform multiple experiments to assess model
performance against various adversarial attack strategies,
measuring key metrics such as accuracy, attack success
rate, F1-score, precision, and recall. Our evaluation involves
training and testing on the CSE-CIC-IDS2018 dataset, which
is widely used for intrusion detection research.
