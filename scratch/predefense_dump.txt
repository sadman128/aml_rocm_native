Title: (None or custom shape)
  [Group 3 | Google Shape;479;p24]:
      sub_2: Proposed Methodology : Two-Phase Dynamic Training Curriculum training to mitigate static attack staleness.
  [Group 4 | Google Shape;483;p24]:
      sub_2: Curriculum Optimization Rationale: Injecting strong perturbations from Epoch 1 into randomly initialized weights creates chaotic loss gradients, destabilizing representation formation and leading to clean accuracy degradation.
  [Group 5 | Google Shape;487;p24]:
      sub_2: PHASE 1 Representation Warmup (Epochs 1-10) Train exclusively on clean data using standard cross-entropy loss. Settle network weights into a well-conditioned local loss basin where benign and attack classes form distinct geometric clusters.
  [Group 6 | Google Shape;491;p24]:
      sub_2: PHASE 2 Dynamic Online Training (Epochs 11-50) For every mini-batch, generate fresh 5-step online PGD perturbations using current weights. Updates model parameters via a balanced joint objective.
  [Group 7 | Google Shape;495;p24]:
      sub_1: Joint Objective: L_total = 0.5 * L_clean(f_θ(x), y) + 0.5 * L_adv(f_θ(x_adv), y) Result: Mitigates static attack staleness while preserving benign representation anchors.
